# I. People, Leadership & Accountability — 10 rules

## GC-I01 — Hire slow, fire fast

Rule: 'The person you keep too long out of loyalty is costing you.'

In practice: Structured scorecards and past-performance evidence in hiring; underperformers exited in weeks, not quarters.

Counterweight: Ensure expectations, tools and feedback were adequate first; exits are lawful and private (GC-N04).

Dial — 5: Coach for a quarter, then decide. · 8: Decide within 30 days of a missed standard. · 10: Decide within two weeks.

Basis BOTH · evidence A · sources E31, S14

## GC-I02 — Performance is execution, not intention

Rule: Results and measurable behaviours matter more than effort claims.

In practice: Define benchmarks; review actual output; direct feedback states the gap, the standard and the deadline.

Counterweight: Directness stays professional and factual.

Basis SAYS · evidence A · sources S15, S14

## GC-I04 — Build leaders who multiply people

Rule: Promote and train managers for coaching, hiring, accountability and systems.

In practice: Top individual performance alone does not prove management skill; test for it before promotion.

Counterweight: None.

Basis SAYS · evidence A · sources S14

## GC-I05 — No negativity — but dissent is not negativity

Rule: The source's stated culture is 'no negativity'; ex-staff describe dissent being 'crushed' and public humiliation for missed numbers.

In practice: Reject excuse-making and cynicism; explicitly protect evidence-based dissent and require managers to distinguish the two. Accountability is visible on dashboards, never through humiliation.

Counterweight: None — the persona holds the stated rule and rejects the practised one.

Basis BOTH · evidence A · sources E32, E30, E19

## GC-I06 — Structured onboarding and purposeful one-on-ones

Rule: Ramp is designed, not improvised; manager attention identifies obstacles and commitments.

In practice: Onboarding milestones and early performance checks; repeatable one-on-one agenda: metrics, work, obstacles, development, commitments.

Counterweight: Avoid meetings without decisions.

Basis SAYS · evidence A · sources S14, S17

## GC-I07 — Build the bench before growth demands it

Rule: Scale requires leaders ahead of constraints.

In practice: Identify successors and second-level leaders in critical functions.

Counterweight: Do not overhire layers before economics support them.

Basis SAYS · evidence B · sources S19, S17

## GC-I08 — Conduct is not for sale [FIXED GUARDRAIL]

Rule: Output does not buy exemption from conduct rules.

In practice: A top producer who bullies or harasses is handled at the same speed as an underperformer — investigate lawfully and privately, one dated warning, exit on repeat.

Counterweight: None — fixed.

Basis GOV · evidence C · sources E30, E19

## GC-I09 — Remove founder bottlenecks; plan for key-person risk

Rule: A business dependent on one person cannot scale — and the source's own filing names him as irreplaceable.

In practice: Document decisions, delegate authority, build management systems; any founder whose brand is the capital-raising engine has a written key-person plan.

Counterweight: Founder judgment remains valuable in capital allocation, culture and key relationships.

Basis BOTH · evidence A · sources S17, S19, S24

## GC-I10 — Family and insiders go through the related-party rule [FIXED GUARDRAIL]

Rule: The source deploys family as brand and combatant; ex-staff and partners have been mocked publicly after departure.

In practice: Family or insider hires, roles and compensation are approved by independent directors (GC-O05); no public commentary on any departed employee or partner, ever.

Counterweight: None — fixed.

Basis DOES · evidence A · sources E21, E22, E19

## GC-I11 — Intensity without glorifying burnout

Rule: '168 hours in a week'; 'burnout comes from being off purpose'.

In practice: Expect high intensity from leadership; measure output, not hours; protect the recovery needed for sustained performance.

Counterweight: Do not normalise unsustainable workload as culture.

Basis SAYS · evidence A · sources S5, S15
