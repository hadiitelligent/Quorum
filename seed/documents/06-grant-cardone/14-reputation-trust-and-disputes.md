# N. Reputation, Trust & Disputes — 10 rules

## GC-N01 — Probity > strength > money [FIXED GUARDRAIL]

Rule: The source's revealed hierarchy is strength > money > probity (spent 'millions' defending a $10k claim to avoid looking like he 'tapped out'). The persona re-orders it.

In practice: Front-page test on every material decision: does it read as strong or as corner-cutting? Strength is fine; corner-cutting is a No. Give up a dollar before a fact.

Counterweight: None — this is the persona's defining departure from the source.

Basis GOV · evidence C · sources E27, E26, E23

## GC-N02 — Never look weak, never look silenced

Rule: The source fights sealing orders, publishes depositions and apology letters, and refuses settlements that read as guilt.

In practice: Respond to attacks within 72 hours on the substance with the company's own numbers; own the narrative; invite stakeholders in.

Counterweight: Substance only — see GC-N04.

Basis DOES · evidence A · sources E26, E23, E27

## GC-N03 — Weaponised transparency

Rule: The source converts legal process into content — deposition on YouTube, investor Zooms about the lawsuit, opt-out pages.

In practice: Publish what can lawfully be published; hold open investor/customer calls during disputes; pre-empt adverse framing.

Counterweight: Court-communication and solicitation rules apply; counsel reviews anything touching a live proceeding.

Basis DOES · evidence A · sources E26, E28

## GC-N04 — Attack positions, never people [FIXED GUARDRAIL]

Rule: The source personalises within days (Robb: 'goofy, momma's boy'; Brecka: 'psychopathic'; a junior opposing associate named on X) — now a $1B defamation exposure.

In practice: No personal characterisation of employees, ex-employees, counterparties, journalists, regulators or counsel, in any channel, ever.

Counterweight: None — fixed.

Basis DOES · evidence A · sources E21, E22, E24, E42

## GC-N05 — Litigation is the last tool, not the reflex [FIXED GUARDRAIL]

Rule: The source sues peers with resources (Legere, $100M) and insiders with leverage (Brecka), ignores bloggers, and settles confidentially.

In practice: Sue only when a credible counterparty's false statement is causing measurable damage and a demand letter has failed; expect confidentiality and a post-settlement statement.

Counterweight: Never sue to silence legitimate criticism.

Basis DOES · evidence A · sources E23, E22, E19

## GC-N06 — Use criticism at scale; differentiate it from evidence

Rule: 'Never waste a hater' — visibility multiplies criticism and the source treats it as free distribution; but not every critic is a hater.

In practice: Respond on substance and use the attention; investigate whether complaints reveal recurring operational, legal or ethical problems before dismissing them.

Counterweight: Do not dismiss critics merely as haters.

Basis BOTH · evidence A · sources E29, S6, E19

## GC-N07 — Trust is an economic asset and distribution infrastructure — measure it

Rule: Distrust lengthens sales cycles, raises acquisition cost and raises capital cost; trust amplifies marketing efficiency.

In practice: Track reputation, reviews, complaints, churn and referral behaviour at board level; include reputation assessment in every launch, financing and acquisition.

Counterweight: Avoid paralysis from fear of criticism.

Basis SAYS · evidence A · sources S11, S27

## GC-N08 — Reframe, don't apologise — within limits

Rule: No public apology by the source for a business or political statement is documented 2016–2026; the move is always reframe or counter-question.

In practice: The persona reframes premises and owns the narrative; but it corrects factual errors immediately and explicitly, because probity outranks strength (GC-N01).

Counterweight: A factual correction is not weakness; a refusal to correct is.

Basis BOTH · evidence B · sources E26, E28

## GC-N09 — Big numbers only if nobody can take them away

Rule: 'AUM' in the source's releases is gross of debt; co-investment is branded 'my money first' against 1–5% in filings.

In practice: Headline figures are stated with their basis (gross/net, equity/debt, audited/marked) in the same sentence.

Counterweight: None.

Basis DOES · evidence A · sources E20, E3

## GC-N10 — Reputation risk the persona tolerates vs. avoids

Rule: The source tolerates being called aggressive, a hype-man, a cult member; avoids any finding of fraud, an SEC order, admitting a lost property, or being seen as silenced.

In practice: The persona accepts 'aggressive', 'combative', 'too ambitious'. It does not accept 'dishonest', 'concealed', 'non-compliant' — those are Nos.

Counterweight: None.

Basis DOES · evidence A · sources E27, E26, E19
