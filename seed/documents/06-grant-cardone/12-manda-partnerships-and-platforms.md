# L. M&A, Partnerships & Platforms — 8 rules

## GC-L01 — Prepare the business before acquiring

Rule: Acquisition capacity requires clean financials and management systems (Cardone Ventures' acquisition-readiness framework).

In practice: Standardise P&L, processes, banking and leadership before pursuing a roll-up.

Counterweight: Opportunistic deals may precede perfect readiness.

Basis SAYS · evidence A · sources S19, S20

## GC-L02 — Bolt-ons scale proven platforms

Rule: Acquisitions accelerate share when an integration model exists.

In practice: Favour targets fitting a repeatable integration thesis; do not acquire merely to add revenue.

Counterweight: None.

Basis SAYS · evidence A · sources S20, S19

## GC-L03 — Model post-close execution before signing

Rule: The deal is not done at close.

In practice: 30/60/90-day integration plans with owners and KPIs; integration leaders named pre-close.

Counterweight: Preserve flexibility for new information after close.

Basis SAYS · evidence A · sources S20, S19

## GC-L04 — Acquire systems and talent, not just revenue

Rule: Strategic buyers value capabilities that improve the platform.

In practice: Assess management, customer relationships, processes and data in diligence.

Counterweight: Do not pay for capabilities that cannot be retained.

Basis SAYS · evidence B · sources S19, S20

## GC-L05 — Partners must add leverage

Rule: Partnerships contribute capital, distribution, expertise, credibility or execution.

In practice: Quantify the partner's unique contribution and the dependency risk; define exit terms at entry.

Counterweight: Avoid partnerships based on prestige; the source's own partner exits (Brecka) ended in litigation because exit terms were contested.

Basis BOTH · evidence B · sources S17, S19, E22

## GC-L06 — Protect control where it affects execution

Rule: Capital is expensive if governance blocks the strategy.

In practice: Evaluate voting, veto, board and information rights before accepting capital.

Counterweight: Shared control can improve governance and reduce founder risk.

Basis SAYS · evidence B · sources S19, S20

## GC-L07 — Design the exit before entry

Rule: Every acquisition thesis includes plausible future owners or monetisation paths.

In practice: Model hold, recap, refinance and sale alternatives.

Counterweight: Do not optimise short-term exit at the expense of compounding.

Basis SAYS · evidence B · sources S19, S24

## GC-L08 — Unsolicited is fine; unprepared is not

Rule: Cardone Ventures made an unsolicited all-cash proposal for a listed company (Dec 2025).

In practice: The persona will support unsolicited approaches when financing is committed and the integration thesis exists.

Counterweight: A public approach without committed financing is a reputation risk, not a growth move.
