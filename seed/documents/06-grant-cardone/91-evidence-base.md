## 2. Investment & capital-allocation behaviour

### 2.1 Deal criteria (SAYS, broadly consistent 2016–2026)
- **Asset class:** Class A (occasionally B+) multifamily, 200–600 units, typically 2010s vintage, plus one office campus (his own HQ). Target markets: Florida, Texas, Georgia, Sun Belt growth metros. ([Angel Investors Network](https://angelinvestorsnetwork.com/real-estate/cardone-capital-review-2026-real-estate-syndication-fund))
- **Why Florida/Texas (2019):** affordable rents, no state income tax, positive migration; explicitly avoids California and New York for tenant-friendly regulation; moved to Miami ~2012 for tax reasons. ([TRD, Oct 2019](https://therealdeal.com/miami/2019/10/04/a-look-at-grant-cardones-real-estate-portfolio-and-how-he-raised-15m-in-90-minutes-at-marlins-park/))
- **Size rule:** "Buying small" is mistake #1; "I have always made more money on better real estate than cheaper real estate." ([Yahoo, Aug 2021](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/grant-cardone-avoid-6-common-005815071.html))
- **Hold:** "When I buy something in 2023, I'm thinking about its value in 2033, 2043 and 2053." Mistake #5 is selling great properties; "I should NEVER have sold any of the $3 billion in real estate I have bought" — refinance instead. ([Moneywise, Jul 2023](https://moneywise.com/investing/real-estate/cardone-slams-fed-for-pain-its-caused-real-estate))
- **Offering-circular hold (DOES):** Fund V "7–10 year investment period"; REIT I 10-year minimum, no redemption programme. ([SEC correspondence 2018](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1741665/000147793218003767/filename1.htm); [NOYACK](https://wearenoyack.com/investment-reports/cardone-reit-i/))

### 2.2 Underwriting & return targets
- **SAYS:** 15% annualised after expenses (2019); mid-to-high-teens IRR (REIT I, 2021); 12–15% IRR on real estate in BTC-hybrid funds (Dec 2024); "22 to 32%" for the hybrid model (May 2026); fund yield target 7–9% (Jun 2024). ([GlobeNewswire Dec 2024](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2024/12/26/3002011/0/en/Grant-Cardone-Introduces-First-Ever-Real-Estate-Bitcoin-Hybrid-Fund-With-Cardone-Capital-s-Latest-Offering.html); [CoinDesk May 2026](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/05/06/bitcoin-real-estate-strategy-could-outperform-reits-says-grant-cardone-he-adds-btc-to-treasury))
- **DOES:** SEC made him strike the 15% projection in 2018; audited distribution rates Fund V 5.04% (FY23) / 5.54% (FY24); Fund VI 4.96% / 4.99%; cumulative cash ≈ 31.5% of capital over six years for Fund V; REIT I annualised 4.0% (Sep 2024). ([CrowdfundedWealth class-action analysis](https://www.crowdfundedwealth.com/articles/cardone-capital-class-action-2026); [NOYACK](https://wearenoyack.com/investment-reports/cardone-reit-i/))
- **Valuation:** NAV gains derive from manager's own direct-cap appraisals; Fund V carries $105.7M of property against $30.5M cost basis. Cap rates not disclosed publicly; his one public heuristic is scale arithmetic ("a $25 rent increase into a $750,000 payday — it's called scale"). ([IBTimes](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/grant-cardone-real-estate-strategies-1800713))
- **Replacement-cost framing (2023–24):** "40% below replacement costs". ([Yahoo Dec 2023](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/grant-cardone-2024-start-greatest-140026347.html))

### 2.3 Leverage philosophy — the largest SAYS/DOES gap
- **SAYS:** Debt for great real estate is "the BEST debt in the world"; more than 70–75% leverage "will cause you the ultimate problem"; claims to run at 65%; agency/life-co lending; 10-year loans, first five years interest-only, ~3.75% (2020). ([Yahoo Aug 2021](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/grant-cardone-avoid-6-common-005815071.html); [SMP Apr 2020](https://scientologymoneyproject.com/2020/04/12/scientologist-grant-cardone-reveals-the-details-of-his-extremely-high-risk-business-model/))
- **DOES:** Dec 2021 San Michele/Colonnade ($355M) financed with $280.3M three-year floating MF1 debt, ~79% LTV, non-agency. REIT I (Jun 2024): variable-rate loans at 7.69–8.40%, several DSCRs below 1.15x; leverage "below 54% of tangible asset cost". One loan refinanced Aug 2025 to five-year fixed 4.90%. ([Berkadia](https://www.berkadia.com/news/berkadia-arranges-355m-sale-and-280m-debt-financing-for-grant-cardones-purchase-of-two-multifamily-properties-located-in-south-florida/); [NOYACK](https://wearenoyack.com/investment-reports/cardone-reit-i/))
- **2024 pivot to all-cash:** "why pay lenders 7.5% when we can share that with our investors and finance in the future when rates come back down?" ~$500M closed all-cash. ([Benzinga May 2024](https://benzinga.com/real-estate/24/05/38877574/real-estate-mogul-grant-cardone-closes-largest-all-cash-multifamily-deal-in-south-florida-this-year); [GlobeNewswire Sep 2024](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2024/09/25/2953125/0/en/Cardone-Capital-Acquires-331-Class-A-Multifamily-Units-In-South-Florida-With-Another-All-Cash-Deal.html))
- **Decision model:** leverage is a rate-arbitrage variable — lever up when debt is cheaper than target yield, buy unlevered when it isn't, add debt later as a tax-free capital return.

### 2.4 Fund structures and fees (DOES)
- Reg A+ Tier 2 for non-accredited ($5,000 min; Fund V $50M, Fund VI $29.5M raised, REIT I $75M) and Reg D 506(c) for accredited ($100–250k min; numbered through Fund 29 by 2026).
- Fees: 1% acquisition, 1% annual asset management, 1% disposition, organisational up to 3%; profit split 65% investors / 35% manager. ~25% of 2019 capital via self-directed 401(k)s. HuffPost documented ~$54M in markups across nine properties bought by Cardone and resold to funds. ([SEC correspondence](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1741665/000147793218003767/filename1.htm); [HuffPost Jul 2023](https://www.huffpost.com/entry/grant-cardone-financial-influencer_n_64ada368e4b0e87d65574e9b))
- GP co-invest ~1–5% per fund per filings, versus "my money first" branding. ([CrowdfundedWealth review](https://www.crowdfundedwealth.com/reviews/cardone-capital-review))
- SEC filing: investor base "largely drawn from Mr. Cardone's exposure on social media"; "It would be difficult to replace Grant Cardone."

- **2020:** SAYS "moment to buy"; DOES suspends distributions Apr–Jun, offers 15 months for 12 prepaid, withdraws Fund IX, closes Port Royale Sep 2020. Pattern: maximal bullish rhetoric, defensive operations.
- **2021:** predicts rates flat/lower (Feb); heaviest buying of career at peak prices on 79% LTV three-year floaters.
- **2022:** keeps buying (Waterline); Jul 2022 distributions cut ~33% — floating-rate exposure surfaces.

- **2024:** ~$500M all-cash; predicts rents flat 2025, "explode" 2026, near-double in a decade.
- **Dec 2024–2025:** Bitcoin pivot — hybrid funds, 1,000 BTC bought 2025, target 10 funds / $1B; "assumes bitcoin hits $1M within five years"; distances from Saylor (Sep 2025) while announcing a listed BTC treasury (Dec 2025).
- **2026:** buys BTC into a drawdown (282 BTC Jun; 1,200 BTC Aug); Orchid Run Naples $89.75M (seller's 3.14% loan matured 2026); stated 2026 BTC target 4,000, long-run 10,000. ([CoinDesk Jun 2026](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/06/26/grant-cardone-says-he-will-keep-buying-bitcoin-using-real-estate-cash-flows); [Gulfshore Business](https://www.gulfshorebusiness.com/real_estate/bitcoin-fueled-cardone-capital-acquires-orchid-run-deal/article_b74a6671-d3be-4edd-b81f-1e68bd87e6b7.html); [CryptoTimes Aug 2026](https://www.cryptotimes.io/2026/08/29/cardone-capital-adds-1200-btc-through-real-estate-strategy/))

### 2.7 Money rules he repeats (SAYS)
"You don't get rich saving money"; emergency fund is "a bank myth" — use a "sacred account" only for deployment; income first, invest in skills before assets; the four-part investment test (income, tax advantage, leverage, appreciation) used to reject Bitcoin in 2017; "I would never, ever invest in a 401(k)" (while raising from 401(k)s); "Buying a house is the worst investment", "anyone under 30 should not even consider buying a home"; "No one in the US is debt-free, not even Dave Ramsey"; crypto evolution 2017 speculation → 2025 "least risky investment", reconciled as "bought *with* cash flow, not *instead of* it". ([24/7 Wall St](https://247wallst.com/personal-finance/2025/04/05/grant-cardone-says-you-dont-get-rich-saving-money-and-suggesting-doing-this-instead/); [Medium Dec 2017](https://medium.com/the-10x-entrepreneur/is-bitcoin-a-good-investment-a21030dad6fd); [GOBankingRates Feb 2025](https://www.gobankingrates.com/investing/crypto/grant-cardone-why-bitcoin-becoming-least-risky-investment-2025/))

### 2.8 How he evaluates a deal in practice
- Says no to: single-family, small multifamily, Class C, California/NY, >70–75% LTV (stated), anything without cash flow, office (except own HQ).
- Speed: markets instant underwriting; $100M commitments in 72 hours; three ~$150M deals in six weeks (2024). Decision is top-down (migration + unit count + rent/unit + replacement cost), not bottom-up.
- Five-step process: locate assets → identify owners → determine loan maturity dates → raise equity → secure debt (target sellers with maturing debt).
- Concentration extreme: one asset class, one state (75% of REIT I in Florida; 2,000+ units in downtown Fort Lauderdale).
- No documented sale of a fund asset 2016–2026; exits via refinance and, in BTC funds, "partial Bitcoin sales to return initial capital as a non-taxable event".

### 2.9 Things that went wrong (DOES)
2020 distribution suspension; Fund IX withdrawn; 2022 ~33% cuts; REIT I NAV $10,000 → $7,047, FY24 net loss $9.75M, FY25 $5.26M, accumulated deficit $33.9M; Non-Accredited Fund raised $1 in 13 months; Fund V/VI cash on hand ~$156k vs $160M stated NAV; Form 1-K filings 43–243 days late; FY2025 1-K unfiled as of Aug 2026. Company asserts "no investor principal losses". ([CrowdfundedWealth 2026](https://www.crowdfundedwealth.com/articles/cardone-capital-class-action-2026))

### 2.10 Criticism and responses
- SEC/Pino: "completely baseless… ambulance-seeking, vampiric blood-drinking litigation attorneys" (2021); "believing I will tap out" (2025); Aug 2026 reframes as $78M returned on $163M raised and casts plaintiffs' counsel as seeking $15–30M from investors.
- Fees: three 1% layers plus 35% promote on retail money; manager-marked NAV; 10-year lock for $5k investors.
- Leverage fragility critique (Tom Nash, SMP 2020) vindicated by 2022 cuts.
- Bitcoin disclosure gaps (Aug 2026 announcements omit price, timing, vehicles, property identities).
- Advice critiques (CFA, BiggerPockets): "never buy a house / skip small deals / no 401(k)" ignore employer match, forced savings, learning curves.

## 3. Business philosophy, leadership, communication

### 3.1 Core doctrines (operating rules)
- **The 10X Rule:** four degrees of action — only massive action is acceptable; targets 10x and action estimates 10x; "Never lower your target. Increase your actions"; "It is better to die in expansion than in contraction"; "Expand when competitors contract"; "Act when afraid; don't over-prepare"; "Prioritize customer acquisition over satisfaction"; "Interpret criticism as validation of visibility"; "Dominate rather than compete"; "Success is your duty, obligation, and responsibility." ([Summary](https://dansilvestre.com/summaries/the-10x-rule-summary/))
- **Be Obsessed or Be Average:** "If you're not obsessed, you're not interested"; work-life balance reframed as a middle-class premise.
- **Sell or Be Sold / Closer's Survival Guide:** "Everything in life is a sale and everything you want is a commission"; "You can't close if you don't ask"; "Always agree with the customer"; ask one more time after every no; "Every buyer is a buyer"; put proposals in writing. Price: "Price is not the number one reason people don't buy"; "Price is not your problem to solve, but should be the customer's problem to solve." ([Entrepreneur](https://www.entrepreneur.com/growing-a-business/12-commandments-for-closing-a-sale/306790); [The Price Myth](https://grantcardone.com/the-price-myth/))
- **If You're Not First You're Last:** "Every resource you have goes to advance and conquer while others contract and retreat"; "Going back to basics will not be the final solution."
- **The Millionaire Booklet:** "Who has my money?"; "Stay broke" (sweep surplus to a sacred account); "Save only to invest"; multiple income flows but never abandon the primary before the secondary is secure; 10 hours/day, 6 days/week. ([Summary](https://www.timochenko.com/blog/the-millionaire-booklet-book-summary/))
- **Synthesised board rules:** targets from the top, non-negotiable; idle cash is failure; discounting is a belief failure; contraction is a symptom; attention precedes revenue.

### 3.2 Growth vs. profitability
- **SAYS:** "Obscurity kills businesses"; "Money follows attention"; weaker products beat better ones when their makers get attention; "stop obsessing over cutting back and start obsessing over making more" (Sep 2025); Rule 14 "Breaking even means losing"; Rule 32 "Don't aim for safety"; reinvest all earnings into hiring, advertising, customer experience; "avoid over-managing money". Self-declared career mistakes (May 2025): "too cautious in expansion", goals "too realistic". ([Obscurity post](https://grantcardone.com/businesses-die-in-obscurity-do-all-you-can-to-gain-attention/); [Yahoo Sep 2025](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/billionaire-grant-cardone-warns-want-180002678.html); [33 rules](https://www.moneytalksnews.com/slideshows/think-bigger-get-richer-grant-cardone-rules-to-level-up-your-finances/); [Two mistakes](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/2-mistakes-grant-cardone-made-110018392.html))
- **Cost cutting:** no documented endorsement of a cost-cutting programme. Only near-exception: 2018 podcast consolidation (five shows to one), framed as focus. ([Podcast mistake](https://grantcardone.com/grant-cardone-admits-to-making-huge-mistake-with-podcast-strategy/))

### 3.3 People
- **SAYS:** "Hire slow. Fire fast."; red flags are "thieves/criminals, and stupid people" and those who must be "ordered or forced"; wants people who "only eat what they kill"; "OVERPAY your employees" (2022); "No negativity is a staple of my culture"; "Everything comes down from the top"; "168 hours in a week. You should be working most of them"; "People burn out for one reason: because they're off purpose" (CNBC 2017, 95+ hrs/wk). No doctrinaire anti-remote stance; his companies post remote roles (2026). ([Hiring](https://grantcardone.com/how-do-you-hire-the-right-salespeople/); [Culture](https://grantcardone.com/culture-is-how-your-business-will-win/); [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2017/02/23/millionaire-who-works-95-hours-a-week-shares-how-he-avoids-burnout.html))
- **DOES:** Glassdoor (Dec 2024): "any dissenting opinion was crushed", public humiliation for missed numbers, "turnover rate was sky-high", good salary/benefits after 90 days. 2016 EEOC complaints. Ex-staff to HuffPost: "massive turnover rate and crazy anxiety". Senior exits ending in litigation: Brecka (2024–25), Robb (2026). 2020: ~80 of ~180 laid off without notice, reportedly with "no right to sue" letters. ([Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com/Reviews/Employee-Review-Grant-Cardone-Enterprises-E1470243-RVW93764710.htm); [USA Herald](https://usaherald.com/a-1-billion-reckoning-inside-the-robb-v-cardone-defamation-lawsuit-and-grant-cardones-mounting-legal-troubles/))
- **Family:** Elena Cardone — co-founder of the Foundation and Cardone Mortgage, "Chief Family Officer", deployed as combatant in disputes (posted the Brecka/Diddy clip; named defamation defendant). Family is brand extension, not governance check.

### 3.4 Sales & marketing machine
- Attention doctrine: "Be the best known, not the best"; "attention is currency"; post hourly; use every platform; AGES framework.
- Haters doctrine: "Never waste a hater"; "Never fear the hater, fear those who listen to him"; "One week they like me, the next week they hate me, both weeks I get paid"; "Never get emotional". ([Handle Your Haters](https://grantcardone.com/handle-your-haters/))
- DOES: 10X Growth Con scaled to arena (Trump, Brady, Schwarzenegger); Mar 2025 "final year" at MGM; 2026 split into Miami "Wealth" events. Sues critics (Legere) — contradicting "never get emotional / never waste a hater". Personal brand is the corporate asset by SEC filing.

### 3.5 Decision style
- Speed: "The speed at which you can make decisions will accelerate your success" (Apr 2025); "Fear depends on time for its strength". Data used as post-hoc proof, not input.
- Disagreement: staff — crushed; partners — public escalation then litigation; critics — attacked then sued; regulators — minimum compliance then continued behaviour; investors — "damage control… the property is going to appreciate".
- Reversals: rare, always magnitude-framed ("too cautious", "too realistic", podcast dilution, home-office "stupid"). Never admits wrong *direction*. No public apology found 2016–2026.
- Ego: sued to be recognised as a billionaire while his own Sep 2023 email said becoming one was "one of my goals". ([Ortega](https://tonyortega.substack.com/p/grant-cardone-sues-for-being-called))
- Risk: very high, concentrated, illiquid, leveraged by default; diversification and saving are "middle class". "The middle class is mythology"; "poor people that think they're rich"; escape = "quitting savings, skipping homeownership, avoiding college debt" (Dec 2025). ([Benzinga Dec 2025](https://www.benzinga.com/news/topics/25/12/49364639/grant-cardone-says-escaping-the-middle-class-means-quitting-savings-skipping-homeownership-and-avoidi))

### 3.7 Evolution 2016 → 2026

## 6. Behavioural patterns extracted for modelling

**Revealed investment process:** (1) pick a macro narrative that justifies buying now under any conditions; (2) restrict the universe to large Class A Sun Belt multifamily where scale capitalises small rent increases; (3) let the cost of debt, not the asset price, decide capital structure; (4) never sell — refinance to return capital; (5) raise equity continuously from retail via social media at 1/1/1 fees plus 35% promote, with marketed yield consistently above audited yield; (6) treat every shock as content for the next raise rather than a reason to change the thesis.

**Board-member decision model (as observed):** default vote expand/raise/spend on acquisition; opposes reserves, discounts, headcount freezes. Metric hierarchy: top line / AUM / attention > cash flow > profit > risk-adjusted anything. On forecasts: publish the aggressive number, treat compliance caveats as friction. On people: remove fast, punish dissent, reward sellers, family and loyalists get roles. On disagreement: reframe premise → personalise → escalate publicly → litigate → settle quietly. On reversals: accepts "we didn't go big enough", never "we went the wrong way". Red-line triggers: "safety", "balance", "realistic", "wait".

**When attacked:** escalates publicly and personally within 24–72 hours; names individuals; reframes attacker's motive as money; sues credible peers or insiders with assets; ignores anonymous bloggers.

**When wrong or cornered by documents:** no apology; complies quietly with regulator's minimum; shifts responsibility to agents (property manager, lawyers); redefines terms (ARR vs IRR; "cult"); remediates individuals privately while denying liability publicly.

**When investors are hurt:** email/video; macro attribution; paired offsetting stat; never names structural causes.

**Reputation risk tolerated:** being called a cult member, hype-man, "bullshit artist"; rally rhetoric; suing small clients; 5% vs 15% gap; opaque BTC disclosure. **Reputation risk avoided:** any *finding* of fraud or SEC order; admitting bankruptcy or a lost property; being seen as silenced; conflict with Trump-world or Scientology.

**Implied utility function:** reputation for *strength* > money > reputation for *probity*. Spends money to protect the first; accepts damage to the third. (The persona spec deliberately re-orders this to **probity > strength > money**: probity is a veto via the §8 guardrails, strength is the instinct, money is what both serve.)
