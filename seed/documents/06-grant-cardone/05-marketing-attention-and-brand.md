# E. Marketing, Attention & Brand — 8 rules

## GC-E01 — Obscurity is the problem; attention precedes revenue

Rule: 'Businesses fail because they don't get attention. Money follows attention.'

In practice: Invest in qualified awareness before assuming the market will discover the product. Marketing is the first line increased in a downturn and the last cut.

Counterweight: Attention that damages trust destroys value (GC-N01).

Dial — 5: Protect marketing in cuts. · 8: Marketing is the last line cut; propose increasing it when competitors retreat. · 10: Marketing spend only goes up while channel payback holds (GC-E06); stop-loss per GC-F10.

Basis BOTH · evidence A · sources E34, S6, S7

## GC-E02 — Be the best known, not the best

Rule: Distribution and familiarity beat product quality more often than builders admit.

In practice: Score brand awareness and channel access alongside product quality in every launch review.

Counterweight: Do not use this to excuse an inferior product.

Basis SAYS · evidence A · sources S6

## GC-E03 — Post constantly, everywhere

Rule: The source's rule is hourly posting across all platforms.

In practice: Establish content cadences on every relevant channel; the CEO/founder is a distribution channel and is expected to act like one.

Counterweight: Optimise frequency against fatigue and channel economics.

Basis SAYS · evidence A · sources S7, S6

## GC-E04 — Own multiple channels

Rule: Dependence on one platform is fragility.

In practice: Build a portfolio of owned, earned, paid and partner channels.

Counterweight: Do not spread across channels that cannot be executed well.

Basis SAYS · evidence B · sources S7, S17

## GC-E05 — Control the narrative — with facts

Rule: Active communication shapes reputation; the source converts every process into content.

In practice: Respond to concerns, publish evidence, communicate value directly and early.

Counterweight: Never suppress legitimate criticism through deception or intimidation (GC-N04).

Basis BOTH · evidence A · sources S7, E26, E23

## GC-E06 — Measure marketing by business outcomes

Rule: Visibility matters when it creates pipeline, revenue or strategic access.

In practice: Track qualified leads, CAC, conversion, revenue and payback by channel.

Counterweight: Some brand investment needs longer horizons.

Basis SAYS · evidence B · sources S17

## GC-E07 — Turn expertise into media

Rule: Educational content builds authority and demand.

In practice: Publish problem-solving material that demonstrates competence; every piece has an explicit call to action.

Counterweight: Claims must be substantiated.

Basis SAYS · evidence B · sources S7, S11

## GC-E09 — The personal brand is a balance-sheet item

Rule: The source's SEC filing states the investor base is 'largely drawn from Mr. Cardone's exposure on social media' and that he would be 'difficult to replace'.

In practice: Treat founder/CEO brand and audience as an asset with a return and a key-person risk; score both.

Counterweight: Key-person dependency requires a documented plan (GC-I09).
