# J. Operations, Systems & Cadence — 8 rules

## GC-J01 — Systems beat heroics

Rule: A scalable company cannot depend on improvisation.

In practice: Document repeatable workflows and decision rights; keep them light enough for speed.

Counterweight: None.

Basis SAYS · evidence A · sources S14, S17

## GC-J02 — Standardise the unit before multiplying it

Rule: Growth replicates a working unit.

In practice: Define core process, economics and controls before adding locations, teams or funds.

Counterweight: Some experimentation precedes standardisation.

Basis SAYS · evidence B · sources S17, S20

## GC-J03 — Know the numbers; lead with leading indicators

Rule: Lagging financials arrive too late to intervene.

In practice: Timely P&L, cash, pipeline, unit economics, utilisation, backlog and cycle times; one accountable owner per metric.

Counterweight: Avoid gameable metrics.

Basis SAYS · evidence A · sources S17, S19, S14

## GC-J04 — Growth must be fulfillable

Rule: Sales growth operations cannot deliver destroys trust.

In practice: Before scaling demand, verify capacity, service levels and working capital.

Counterweight: Temporary strain is acceptable with a credible ramp plan.

Basis SAYS · evidence B · sources S16, S17

## GC-J05 — Operating cadence: daily / weekly / monthly

Rule: Execution improves with structured review cycles.

In practice: Daily activity, weekly pipeline and cash, monthly P&L and strategy; every review ends with owner, action, date.

Counterweight: Do not create meeting overload.

Basis SAYS · evidence B · sources S14, S17

## GC-J06 — Technology as leverage

Rule: Automation where it removes bottlenecks or improves customer economics.

In practice: Prioritise by bottleneck removed, not by novelty.

Counterweight: No technology projects without measurable business value.

Basis SAYS · evidence A · sources S17

## GC-J07 — Document enough to survive turnover and acquisition

Rule: Institutional knowledge should not leave with people.

In practice: SOPs, reporting definitions and role playbooks for critical processes.

Counterweight: Focus documentation on high-value processes.

Basis SAYS · evidence B · sources S19, S20

## GC-J08 — Time follows priorities

Rule: Many time constraints are allocation problems.

In practice: Audit leadership calendars against declared priorities quarterly; delegate repeatable work; kill low-return meetings and reports.

Counterweight: Retain necessary compliance and maintenance work.

Basis SAYS · evidence A · sources S5, S17
