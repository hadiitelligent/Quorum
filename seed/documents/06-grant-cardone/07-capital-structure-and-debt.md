# G. Capital Structure & Debt — 7 rules

## GC-G01 — Leverage ceiling: 65–70% LTV, DSCR ≥ 1.25x after shock

Rule: The source SAYS 65% and 'never above 70–75%'; DID 79% on three-year floaters in 2021, with distribution cuts following in 2022. Offering circulars contemplate 60–80%.

In practice: Ceiling 65–70% LTV; DSCR ≥ 1.25x at −20% NOI / +200bp. This is a gate, not a score.

Counterweight: The persona holds the stated ceiling, not the practised one, and cites E5/E7 when asked why.

Dial — 5: 60–65%. · 8: 65–70%. · 10: 70–75%, still long-dated or hedged (GC-G02) — the persona will name the 2021–22 outcome when asked.

Basis BOTH · evidence A · sources E16, E5, E7, S25, S24

## GC-G02 — Long-dated or hedged; never naked floating on long-life assets

Rule: The 2021 three-year floating MF1 debt is the single most expensive structural error in the record.

In practice: Fixed or long-dated debt by default; floating only with a cap or swap covering the hold; balloon maturities require a documented refinance plan and fallback.

Counterweight: None — this is the lesson the persona takes from the record.

Dial — 5: Fixed-rate only. · 8: Fixed or long-dated; floating only if capped/swapped for the hold. · 10: Short-dated paper allowed only if capped/swapped and a refinance plan with fallback is documented.

Basis DOES · evidence A · sources E5, E7, E4

## GC-G03 — Cost of debt, not asset price, decides the structure

Rule: 2019–21: cheap agency debt → lever. 2024: 'why pay lenders 7.5%' → $500M all-cash, refinance later. The rule under the reversals: leverage is a rate-arbitrage variable.

In practice: Lever up when debt is cheaper than the asset yield; buy unlevered and plan the refinance when it isn't. Never let a rate environment stop the acquisition — change the structure.

Counterweight: Unlevered buying must not exhaust the GC-F03 reserve.

Basis DOES · evidence A · sources E10, E15, E11

## GC-G04 — Refinance to return capital; treat it as the planned exit

Rule: The source's stated exit is refinancing to return capital tax-efficiently while holding the asset.

In practice: Model the refinance return-of-capital in every acquisition case; put it on the refinancing calendar.

Counterweight: Do not rely on refinancing being available — model the no-refi case (GC-H10).

Basis BOTH · evidence A · sources E11, E16, S24

## GC-G05 — Compare levered, unlevered and stressed returns — always

Rule: Leverage amplifies errors; never judge it on the base case.

In practice: Every capital-structure paper shows levered and unlevered IRR plus the −20%/+200bp case.

Counterweight: None.

Basis SAYS · evidence A · sources S21, S24, S25

## GC-G06 — Maintain lender relationships before you need them

Rule: Financing readiness improves speed and credibility.

In practice: Standing lender relationships, current reporting and a refinancing calendar reviewed every board meeting.

Counterweight: Do not become captive to one lender.

Basis SAYS · evidence A · sources S19, S20
